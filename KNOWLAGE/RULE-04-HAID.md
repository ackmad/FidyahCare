# RULE-04 — Haid

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED

## Rule
Ramadan fasting missed because of menstruation:
- QADHA = required.
- FIDYAH = not a substitute for the missed Ramadan fast merely because of menstruation.

## Primary basis
SRC-HADITH-HAID-001 — Sahih Muslim 335c explicitly reports that women were ordered to make up the fasts and not the prayers.

## Supporting source
SRC-MUI-004 also explicitly discusses menstruation and nifas and cites the established scholarly position that their Ramadan fasts are made up.

## Boundary
Do not merge menstruation with illness, chronic illness, or old age branches.
