# RULE-12 — Sengaja Tidak Berpuasa / Pembatalan Tanpa Uzur

Status: GREEN — VERIFIED, CLASSIFICATION-ONLY

## Scope
This rule classifies the situation; it does not attempt to generate a universal kaffarah/fidyah ruling for every intentional violation.

## Core principle
Do not collapse:
- intentionally not fasting from the start;
- intentionally breaking a fast;
- eating/drinking;
- sexual intercourse during a Ramadan fast;
into one legal outcome.

Different acts and circumstances can have different consequences.

## Kaffarah warning
The well-known sahih hadith concerning a man who had intercourse during Ramadan establishes a specific kaffarah case. This must not be generalized to every broken fast.

## Application
If the user selects "sengaja tanpa uzur":
→ OUTSIDE GENERAL FIDYAH CALCULATOR
→ explain that the case requires a specific fiqh determination.
→ if the user describes sexual intercourse during a Ramadan fast, route to a dedicated kaffarah source/rule rather than the fidyah calculator.

## Safety
Never tell the user that fidyah alone automatically resolves an intentional violation.
