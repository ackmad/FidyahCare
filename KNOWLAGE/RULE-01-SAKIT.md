# RULE-01 — Sakit Sementara

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED

## Scope
User is temporarily ill and reasonably expected to recover enough to fast later.

## Rule
If fasting is missed because of illness within the documented scope:
- QADHA = required for the missed days.
- FIDYAH = not a substitute merely because the person was temporarily ill.

## Basis
- SRC-QURAN-001
- SRC-QURAN-002
- SRC-MUI-001

## Guardrail
The app must not diagnose illness. Ask the user whether they were unable to fast due to illness and whether the condition is temporary/expected to improve. For medical uncertainty, recommend medical advice.
