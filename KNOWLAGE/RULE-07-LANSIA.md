# RULE-07 — Lansia

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED

## Rule
An elderly person who is genuinely unable to fast on a continuing/permanent basis may enter the fidyah framework.

## Evidence
- SRC-ATHAR-IBNABBAS-001 — Ibn Abbas, Sahih al-Bukhari 4505.
- SRC-QURAN-001.
- SRC-BAZNAS-001 as institutional explanation.

## Guardrail
Age alone is not the rule.

Do not implement:
age >= X → fidyah.

The relevant condition is inability to fast in a continuing/permanent sense.
