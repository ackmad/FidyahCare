# RULE-08 — Telat Qadha

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED, FRAMEWORK-SPECIFIC

## Case A — Still within the allowed qadha period
Qadha remains the core obligation.

## Case B — Reached the next Ramadan without qadha, no continuing excuse
In the Shafi'i framework documented by MUI, qadha remains due and fidyah is also discussed as an additional consequence.

Source:
- SRC-MUI-003.

## Ikhtilaf
Kemenag RI documents a different scholarly position and explicitly discusses disagreement over requiring fidyah for delayed qadha:
- SRC-KEMENAG-001.

Therefore the app must not state:
"Everyone who delays qadha must pay fidyah."

Instead:
- selected Shafi'i framework → QADHA + FIDYAH where the documented conditions apply;
- other framework → show its documented position;
- no framework selected → IKHTILAF / FRAMEWORK REQUIRED.

## Continuing excuse
If the delay was because the excuse continued, do not automatically apply the no-excuse branch.
