# ANTIGRAVITY RULES — v2.3

## 1. Source lock
Do not browse the open web to invent or discover a new religious ruling during normal runtime.

Use SOURCE_REGISTRY and the rule files.

## 2. No invented law
AI must never:
- invent a verse;
- invent a hadith;
- invent a hadith grade;
- invent a scholar position;
- invent a numeric conversion;
- silently combine different madhhab positions.

## 3. Source hierarchy
Qur'an → Hadis Nabi ﷺ → Atsar → Tafsir/Syarah → Fiqh/Mazhab → Institutional guidance → application.

## 4. Institutional sources
MUI, Kemenag, BAZNAS, and NU may document a framework or operational guidance. They are not to be labelled as Qur'an/Hadis.

## 5. GREEN semantics
GREEN = source-verified and bounded.
GREEN does not mean consensus.
GREEN does not mean automatically resolvable.

## 6. Medical safety
No diagnosis. No prognosis generated by AI. Ask for user-provided condition context and recommend qualified medical advice for medical uncertainty.

## 7. Ikhtilaf
When the outcome materially differs between documented positions:
- show the disagreement;
- identify the framework;
- never hide it.

## 8. Traceability
Every legal outcome must have:
user_answers → rule_id → framework_id if relevant → source_ids → explanation.

## 9. Insufficient evidence
If a required source or rule is missing:
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE → HUMAN_REVIEW.
Do not use free web search as a substitute for the missing rule.

## 10. Calculator
Never hard-code:
- universal 675 g;
- universal travel distance;
- universal rupiah amount;
- universal permission/prohibition of monetary fidyah.

## 11. Advanced cases
Meninggal and intentional violations are GREEN knowledge modules but remain conditional/review-gated in the automated engine.

## 12. UI language
Never say:
"Ini pasti hukum Islam."

Prefer:
"Dalam kerangka yang dipilih..."
"Berdasarkan sumber yang digunakan..."
"Terdapat perbedaan pendapat..."
