# FOUNDATION-001 — Primary Evidence Framework

Status: GREEN — VERIFIED

## Principle
QS Al-Baqarah 2:184–185 is the principal Qur'anic foundation for the Ramadan fasting concessions discussed in this MVP.

The Qur'an gives the foundation for:
- illness and travel leading to making up missed days;
- fidyah in the category described by the verse;
- the principle that Allah intends ease, not hardship.

## Important interpretation boundary
The Qur'an does not itself spell out every modern application branch, every measurement conversion, or every disagreement among madhhabs.

Therefore:

Qur'an → Hadis/Atsar → Tafsir/Syarah → Fiqh/Mazhab → application rule.

## Prohibited shortcut
Do not transform:
"QS 2:184 mentions fidyah"
into:
"Every person who misses fasting pays fidyah."

That conclusion would ignore the qadha rule in the same passage and the detailed fiqh discussion.
