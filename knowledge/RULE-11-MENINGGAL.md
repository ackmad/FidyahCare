# RULE-11 — Meninggal dengan Utang Puasa

Status: GREEN — VERIFIED, CONDITIONAL / ADVANCED

## Why GREEN does not mean automatic
The primary hadith evidence is authentic, but fiqh application contains important interpretive distinctions. This rule is therefore verified knowledge but must not silently produce one universal legal outcome.

## Primary evidence
- SRC-HADITH-DECEASED-001 — Bukhari 1952.
- SRC-HADITH-DECEASED-002 — Bukhari 1953.
- SRC-HADITH-DECEASED-003 — Muslim 1147.
- SRC-HADITH-DECEASED-004 — Muslim 1148a.

These reports establish a basis for making up certain fasts on behalf of a deceased person.

## Institutional fiqh discussion
Kemenag documents more than one scholarly approach and discusses the evidence used for fidyah versus qadha:
- SRC-KEMENAG-001.

## Application rule
The app must first distinguish:
1. Was the missed fasting obligation one that remained due until death?
2. Was the person continuously excused until death?
3. Which fiqh framework is being followed?

If the framework is not selected:
→ show VERIFIED IKHTILAF / FRAMEWORK REQUIRED.

Do not automatically output:
"pay fidyah"
or
"family must fast"
without the framework and case distinction.

## MVP recommendation
This rule may be displayed as an educational advanced case, but the default automated decision engine should route complex cases to HUMAN_REVIEW.
