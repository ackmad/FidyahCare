# RULE-06 — Hamil / Menyusui

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED, IKHTILAF PRESERVED

## Framework
The application uses an explicit Shafi'i framework when the user selects it.

## Shafi'i framework documented by MUI
- Fear for own health → QADHA.
- Fear for own health and child → QADHA.
- Fear for child only → QADHA + FIDYAH.

Source: SRC-MUI-002.

## Ikhtilaf
Other madhhabs/scholars differ in details. The app must show the selected framework and must not phrase the Shafi'i result as universal consensus.

## Medical boundary
Do not diagnose risk to mother or child. The app may ask what condition/framework the user is applying and should recommend medical advice where health risk is uncertain.
