# RULE-05 — Nifas

Status: GREEN — FINAL VERIFIED

## Rule
A woman experiencing nifas does not fast Ramadan during the relevant condition; the missed Ramadan fasts are made up (qadha) afterward.

## Sources
- SRC-MUI-004 — MUI discussion explicitly covers both haid and nifas and states the obligation of qadha.
- SRC-QURAN-001/002 — general Ramadan concession framework.
- Scholarly support cited by MUI: Imam al-Nawawi, as quoted in the MUI article.

## Important boundary
Do NOT hard-code "nifas = 40 days" as a universal rule.

The application only needs to determine:
- whether the user is currently in nifas;
- how many Ramadan fasting days were missed;
- whether those days have been made up.

## Output
QADHA.
NO_AUTOMATIC_FIDYAH.
